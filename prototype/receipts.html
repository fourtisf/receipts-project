How it works

Three transactions. One receipt.

Click a step to see where it lands on the receipt.

  1. 1

    Commit

    The strategy posts a fingerprint of its next trade. Nobody can read it yet, including us.

    commit(hash(asset, side, size, salt))
  2. 2

    Execute

    Your vault swaps through a pinned Uniswap pool. Fills worse than Chainlink minus your slippage limit are rejected.

    vault.swap(NVDA, BUY, 412.50 USDG)
  3. 3

    Reveal

    The strategy publishes the decision and its salt. The contract checks it matches and stamps the receipt.

    reveal(signal, salt) == commit

Security

Your vault sets the rules.

Strategies only trade inside the limits you choose. These are enforced by the contract, not by us.

  • Oracle price check

    Fills worse than the Chainlink price minus your slippage limit revert.

  • Daily limit

    Caps the dollar value a strategy can trade from your vault in 24 hours.

  • Drawdown breaker

    Trading pauses by itself when the vault falls a set percentage from its peak.

  • Withdraw any time

    Works while paused, and directly from Blockscout if this website is offline.

  • Revoke the bot

    One transaction removes trading access completely.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Can the bot take my money?

No. The vault contract has no function that lets the bot send funds out. It can only swap between the assets you allowed, within your daily limit and slippage limit. Only your wallet can withdraw.

What is a missed reveal?

A strategy commits a fingerprint before each trade and must publish the decision behind it within a time window. If it doesn’t, the receipt is marked missed, and the count stays on the strategy’s page permanently.

How can an AI strategy be verified?

Each signal from an AI strategy also commits the model name and a hash of the exact prompt, so anyone can check that neither changed silently.

What does Receipts charge?

Nothing on deposits, withdrawals or swaps beyond gas and the Uniswap pool fee. Creators earn a share of profit above your vault’s previous high, and Receipts keeps part of that share.

What if this website goes offline?

Your vault lives on Robinhood Chain, not on our servers. You can call withdraw directly from Blockscout at any time.
